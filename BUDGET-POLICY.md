# Barbarossa operating budget policy

Approved owner policy (2026-10-26). This document specifies future eligibility; it does **not** activate paid execution.

## Stages

1. **Startup**: maximum **$100 USD per calendar month** across all Barbarossa and Son of Dragon operating costs; preferred spending **$0**. No unapproved purchases, subscriptions or renewals.
2. **Revenue growth**: eligible only after **three consecutive completed calendar months** with **at least $500 each month in collected sales after refunds and processing/platform fees**. Maximum operating budget is **25% of the previous completed month's qualifying collected sales after deductions**. Eligibility and the amount must be verified and approved by the owner before changing the active limit.
3. **Established profitability**: once **verified monthly net profit exceeds $10,000** (strictly greater than $10,000), the maximum operating budget becomes **10% of verified monthly net profit**. Net profit is after all operating costs, agent fees, refunds and processing charges. This transition also requires owner approval.

## Safeguards

- A cap is a maximum, **not** permission to spend. Owner approval is required for every payment, subscription, budget increase, and major financial decision.
- All hosting, AI API calls, storage, agent collaboration, automation, subscriptions and other operating costs are included in the aggregate monthly cap. Any internal agent-specific allowance is a sublimit, not additional money.
- Recalculate limits using **verified completed-period data**. If revenue or profit declines, lower the permitted spending accordingly; never retain a higher allowance without continued eligibility and approval. At zero or negative qualifying net profit in the profitability stage, no new spending is allowed.
- Prevent double counting of sales, expenses, refunds and fees. Verify receipts and reconcile against provider billing.
- No agent can waive the rules, change its own approval authority, or bypass Global Pause.
- Until provider-side billing safeguards, accounting reconciliation, transaction-safe reservations, owner approval and execution gates are independently verified, **Global Pause stays ON and external spending remains disabled**.
- **Current enforcement remains $100/month in the database**. The dynamic stage policy is documented but **not implemented or activated**. Do not change the existing hard-coded cap based on unverified sales figures.
