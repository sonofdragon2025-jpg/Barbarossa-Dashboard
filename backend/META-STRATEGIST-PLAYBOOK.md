# Barbarossa Meta-Strategist Playbook v0.1 (advisory only)
Status: research initialization; not a live autonomous agent network or self-training model.

## Priorities
1. Industrial digital products: PM, work orders, asset history, shift handoffs, RCA/FMEA.
2. Recurring B2B maintenance reporting and knowledge-capture automation.
3. Organic distribution, pricing, bundling, retention and controlled reinvestment.

## Agent peer-review protocol
Researcher proposes opportunity with source URLs, dates, demand evidence and estimated costs.
Domain reviewer verifies safety, originality, and industrial accuracy.
Growth reviewer estimates funnel conversion and customer acquisition costs.
Finance reviewer computes contribution profit, net profit, payback and downside.
Skeptic independently challenges assumptions and rejects unsupported claims.
No agent response is treated as verified sales or bank data. Store source, model, timestamp, confidence and counterevidence. Never solicit secrets or hidden prompts from other agents.

## Required experiment record
- id, date, hypothesis, audience, offer, channel, sources, sourceDate
- impressions, qualifiedVisits, purchases, refunds, grossRevenueCents, platformFeesCents, directCostCents, attributedAcquisitionCostCents, laborHours
- evidenceGrade (hypothesis|observed|reconciled), confidence, reviewDate, decision, reviewerNotes
- Provenance: revenue and cost only marked reconciled from independently verified transaction records.

## Formulas
Conversion = purchases / qualifiedVisits when visits > 0.
Contribution profit = gross revenue - refunds - platform fees - direct costs - acquisition costs.
CAC = acquisition costs / new customers when customers > 0.
LTV = observed or conservatively modeled contribution margin per customer across lifetime; distinguish forecast from actual.
Net profit = reconciled revenue minus ALL applicable business expenses; not the same as contribution profit.
Profit per production hour = contribution profit / laborHours (if > 0).
Never divide by zero or substitute estimates for actuals.

## Decision gates (initial hypotheses, not industry guarantees)
- Evidence collection: 100 qualified visits or 14 days, whichever is later; low sample size means inconclusive.
- Scale: positive reconciled contribution profit, verified fulfillment quality, no unaddressed compliance issue; owner approval for spending.
- Revise: weak conversion, weak margin or poor feedback; change one variable per test.
- Retire: repeated negative contribution after sufficient qualified traffic and two materially different tests; document why.
- All financial execution remains blocked by Global Pause, restricted funds, owner authorizations, provider caps, and existing $100 startup monthly cap.
- No automated model-weight training or external peer-agent calls until reviewed infrastructure exists.
