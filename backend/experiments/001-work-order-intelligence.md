# Experiment 001: Work Order Intelligence Toolkit
Status: research specification only; no verified sales and no publication.
Owner: Hiott Holding LLC

## Hypothesis
Maintenance teams may pay for a practical toolkit that makes the last three asset failures visible before work begins and makes technician closeout notes useful to the next shift.

## Proposed deliverables
- Editable three-failure history worksheet
- Pre-job context and safety review checklist
- Technician closeout template
- CSV history schema with synthetic examples
- Supervisor repeat-failure review guide

## Initial measurement
Record qualified visits, purchases, refunds, revenue, platform fees, direct costs, labor hours, and sources. Mark estimates separately from reconciled observations. Evaluate after at least 100 qualified visits and 14 days; insufficient data means inconclusive.

## Reviews before publication
Industrial accuracy and safety; originality; document rendering; CSV validity; product download verification; pricing; payment safeguards.

## Controls
No wallet transactions, PayPal payouts, paid advertising, live publishing or spending authorized by this experiment. Existing Global Pause and startup budget restrictions remain in force.
