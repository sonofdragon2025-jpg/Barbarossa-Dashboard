1/3 A spare that has not moved in 18 months can still be your most important part. Usage alone misses consequence: What stops if it fails? How long is the lead time? Is there a tested alternate? Start with risk, not just inventory turns.

2/3 For repeat-use parts, a simple review trigger is average daily usage x (supplier lead days + buffer days). But zero usage on a critical PLC, drive or safety-related component should trigger engineering review - not an automatic zero-stock recommendation.

3/3 Make the weekly spares meeting 15 minutes: confirm counts, check open orders and other sites, validate supplier lead times, and log who approves each decision. A spreadsheet can propose a reorder; only your purchasing process can authorize one.
