## Stop treating every spare part like a routine consumable.

A rarely used PLC power supply can matter more to uptime than a fast-moving box of belts. Many storerooms still rely on incomplete counts, old lead times and generic min/max rules.

The **Critical Spares Decision Kit** is a practical review system for maintenance managers, planners and storeroom leads to identify critical gaps, data problems, engineering-review items and suggested reorder quantities **before an outage turns into an expedite**.

### Included

- Five-sheet Excel workbook: START HERE, Spare Inputs, Reorder Review, Dashboard, Decision Log.
- Formula-driven available stock, reorder point, target stock, suggested quantity and estimated product cost.
- Exception flags for critical gaps, zero-demand engineering reviews and inconsistent records.
- Seven-page PDF guide with worked example, 15-minute weekly review and 30-day rollout.
- Five clearly labeled synthetic sample records to replace before use.

**Important:** No macros, logins, subscriptions or automatic purchases. Recommendations are review-only; verify physical counts, supplier lead times, interchangeability and management approvals. Not an ERP, engineering certification, safety procedure or guarantee of downtime reduction.
