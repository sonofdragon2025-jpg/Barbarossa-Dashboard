# Barbarossa 30-day soft-launch operating plan

**Scope:** Hiott Holding LLC / Payhip. **Hard cap:** $100/month, with no automatic cap increase, payment, or unverified publication. Existing published products are not to be modified. Global Pause and provider-side controls remain authoritative.

## Product portfolio: maximum 10, prioritize five
1. Maintenance Shift Handoff Template (existing; verify current listing and downloadable files)
2. PM Reliability Starter Toolkit (existing; verify current listing and downloadable files)
3. Repeat-Failure / Last-Three-Work-Orders Investigation Worksheet
4. Preventive Maintenance Compliance and Backlog Dashboard
5. Root Cause Analysis (5-Why + Fishbone) Field Kit
6. Spare Parts Criticality and Min/Max Planner
7. Maintenance Skills Matrix and Training Handoff
8. BAS/SCADA Alarm Response and Change-Control Log
9. Critical Facilities Equipment Readiness Checklist
10. Maintenance Weekly KPI Review Pack

Products 3–10 are candidates, **not** claims of existing listings. Start with five, validate demand and differentiation before expanding. Each requires original technical content, accurate safety caveats, real DOCX/CSV bytes, independent QA, downloadable-file hash match, and recorded price.

## Payhip upload acceptance
- Verify the saved authenticated seller session belongs to the intended storefront.
- Inspect live Payhip UI selectors; record verified contract and account identity.
- Upload one reviewed **invisible** test draft, persist the product ID before any retry, then download the actual customer files and compare SHA-256 hashes.
- Verify title, price, description, visibility and deliverability. On uncertainty stop, reconcile by product ID, and do not create duplicates.
- Do not make products public without the current release approval policy and all verified financial and operational controls. Do not bypass Global Pause to complete a test.

## Marketing: content first, no unsolicited bulk messaging
- 3 original educational posts per week (12/month), with practical maintenance lessons, one optional product link and no invented claims.
- One weekly guide on repeat failures, PM compliance, closeout quality, or BAS alarm troubleshooting.
- Draft personalized outreach to 10 qualified plant/maintenance/reliability contacts per week (40/month); verify contact role and organization before any outreach and follow applicable anti-spam requirements.
- Record channel/source tags and visits; do not claim tracking exists until connected and verified.

## Financial instrumentation
Record daily actual orders, gross revenue, refunds, Payhip fees, processor fees, chargebacks, cloud and model spend, browser spend, and visits. Distinguish verified source data from estimates. Use backend/soft-launch-metrics.js for *estimates*, never as a payment authorization. Report conversion only when visits are known. Reinvestment requires positive reconciled operating profit, verified cash availability, current provider billing caps, and explicit owner approval. Never infer sales from listings or traffic.

## Weekly decision gates
- Week 1: verify Payhip connection and draft delivery, audit two existing products, establish measured costs and analytics.
- Week 2: QA and draft three high-value candidate products, publish only after release gates.
- Week 3: run educational posts and tracked outreach; monitor actual visits and customer feedback.
- Week 4: reconcile sales, fees, refunds, costs; decide whether to expand the portfolio. No budget increase without owner approval.

**Failure behavior:** stop external operations if credentials, delivery, billing caps, durable ledger, customer metrics, or session validity cannot be verified. Continue offline product drafting and QA without paid or public side effects.
