## Landing page draft
**Waiting Parts & Vendor Follow-up Kit — $19.00**

Stop losing track of maintenance work orders while waiting for parts, service vendors, or approvals. An editable five-tab Excel workbook and practical PDF guide for maintenance supervisors, planners and facilities managers. Track each blocked work order's internal owner, follow-up date, supplier commitment, evidence, and escalation. Includes Open Follow-ups, Escalation Matrix, Contact Log, Weekly Review, and Instructions. Fictional example rows are included and must be deleted before use. Supplements, but does not replace, your CMMS or site procedures. No automatic vendor contact, compliance certification or guaranteed downtime savings.

## Three-post educational X draft
1. “Waiting on parts” is not a maintenance plan. An open work order needs an owner, a next-contact date, and evidence of what the vendor actually committed to. Otherwise it disappears into the backlog.
2. Try a 10-minute daily blocked-work-order review: filter overdue follow-ups, call the responsible supplier or approver, record the confirmed next action, and update the CMMS. Prioritize safety and production consequences under site policy.
3. Measure the right thing: not just how many work orders are “awaiting parts,” but how many have a named owner and a dated next action. A simple follow-up register can make gaps visible. What field is most often missing from your work orders?
