**Title:** Repeat Failure Evidence-to-Action Kit | Maintenance RCA Starter Templates
**Price:** $19.00 USD (proposed, not live)
**Hook:** Stop calling a repeat breakdown “fixed” just because the machine restarted.
**Description:** Turn scattered corrective work orders into a consistent repeat-failure review. This editable text-based kit provides a CSV-ready failure-history register, repeat-event triage, evidence-versus-hypothesis worksheet, corrective-action tracker, 30/60-day effectiveness review guidance, and a weekly management agenda. **Audience:** Industrial maintenance managers, reliability leads, supervisors and facilities teams.
**CTA:** Build your first evidence-backed repeat-failure review.
**Safety:** Review all corrective actions under your facility's safety and change-control processes.

## Three-post educational X thread (draft; not posted)
**1/3** A machine restarting is not the same as a failure being solved. If the same asset breaks again, compare the last three work orders: failure mode, evidence, restoration action, and what was never tested. The gaps often tell you what to investigate next.
**2/3** Keep facts separate from theories. “Belt slipped three times” is an observation. “Misalignment caused it” is a hypothesis until measurements support it. Record what evidence could prove *or disprove* the suspected cause.
**3/3** Close the loop with an owner, due date, approved corrective action and effectiveness check. Measure recurrence over a defined window; don't equate a completed work order with verified prevention. #Maintenance #Reliability #RCA
