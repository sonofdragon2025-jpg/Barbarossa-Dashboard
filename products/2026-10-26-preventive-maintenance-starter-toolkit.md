## Purpose
A practical starter pack for manufacturing and facilities teams to organize preventive maintenance, assign ownership, prioritize assets, and measure execution. Customize for site-specific hazards and OEM requirements.

## 2. Criticality Score
Rate each 1-5 (1=low, 5=high):
- Safety/environmental impact
- Production/service impact
- Repair lead time and spare availability
- Failure frequency
- Quality/compliance impact

**Total score:** 5-25. Suggested priorities: 20-25 critical, 13-19 high, 8-12 medium, 5-7 low. Validate cutoffs with your own risk assessment.

## 3. PM Work Order Template
- Work order number / asset ID / technician / date
- Required LOTO and permits (per site procedure)
- OEM instructions and approved checklist revision
- Pre-work condition, abnormalities, measurements
- Inspection / lubrication / adjustment tasks
- Parts and labor hours
- Defects found; corrective work order reference
- Functional test and return-to-service authorization
- Completion timestamp and supervisor review

## 4. Weekly Planning Checklist
- [ ] Review overdue critical-asset PMs
- [ ] Confirm production access and lockout windows
- [ ] Check parts, tools, permits and vendor requirements
- [ ] Assign qualified technicians and realistic labor hours
- [ ] Separate planned corrective work from emergency work
- [ ] Verify work completion and capture findings
- [ ] Review repeat failures and PM effectiveness

## 5. KPI Definitions
- **PM Compliance** = PM work orders completed on time / PM work orders due × 100.
- **Schedule Compliance** = Scheduled work orders completed as planned / work orders scheduled × 100.
- **Emergency Work %** = Emergency labor hours / total maintenance labor hours × 100.
- **MTTR** = Total corrective repair time / number of repair events.
- **MTBF** = Total operating time / number of failures (for repairable assets).

Define exclusions, work-order categories, and measurement periods consistently.

## 6. 30-Day Launch Plan
**Week 1:** Inventory critical assets; agree on ownership and definitions.
**Week 2:** Verify OEM PM tasks, hazards, spare parts and realistic intervals.
**Week 3:** Build and assign CMMS work orders; pilot on critical assets.
**Week 4:** Audit work quality, capture defects, baseline KPIs and adjust.

## Notes
This toolkit is an editable starting point, not a substitute for OEM procedures, qualified personnel, site safety policies, or regulatory requirements. Always use approved LOTO procedures and facility-specific hazard assessments.
