# Autonomous clips workflow

Owner authorization, 2026-10-27. Barbarossa may research online sources, create original clips, choose titles and descriptions, schedule and publish without daily approval, within the existing budget and quality requirements. This extends AUTONOMOUS-MANDATE.md explicitly to online-source clips.

## Operating sequence

1. Research demand and rank relevant topics. Start with maintenance, equipment, reliability and leadership to support the existing Hiott Holding LLC catalog; expand based on measured results.
2. Select assets with documented permission for commercial reuse and editing, or verified public-domain status. Verify every component including music, footage and images, attribution conditions and platform scope. Source popularity or download availability is not permission. Do not bypass access restrictions.
3. Skip unclear or incompatible sources automatically and select an alternative. Fair-use-only material is not an unattended publishing source; retain it for separate review rather than treating an AI assertion as clearance.
4. Produce an original script, meaningful explanation/commentary, captions, properly licensed audio and a rendered vertical clip. Avoid minimally changed reposts and repetitive bulk content. Rights clearance and monetization eligibility are separate checks.
5. An independent worker checks the actual rendered artifact, source evidence, facts, safety, audio, caption accuracy, attribution, disclosures and target-platform requirements. Record file hashes, source/license evidence and reviewer identity. No model self-approval as the sole gate.
6. Publish automatically through an authenticated supported integration to the verified business account. Do not ask daily. Reserve any billable obligations before execution under the shared budget and 30-day runway rule.
7. Use a durable per-artifact/per-destination idempotency key. If an upload times out, query the platform for its result before retrying. A scheduled post is not a confirmed public post. Record actual post IDs, URLs, publication state and matching media.
8. Collect actual reach, retention, clicks, sales, platform payouts and total production/distribution costs when connected. Missing metrics remain unknown. Reuse the 24-hour performance review and seven-day experiment rules. Do not buy views, spam comments or promise income.
